Put your photos in this folder.

To use a photo, open index.html or services.html, find a placeholder like:
  <div class="ph c1" role="img" aria-label="..."><span class="cap">Add photo</span></div>
and replace it with:
  <div class="ph c1"><img src="images/your-photo.jpg" alt="Describe the photo"></div>

Tips: use JPG or WebP, about 1200px wide, under 300 KB each.
